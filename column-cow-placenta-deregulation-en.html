Why Bovine Placenta Became Usable in Japan Again — The Road to the 2018 Regulatory Reform

Published: 2026-08-18 | DARD Co., Ltd.

"Is bovine placenta permitted for use in Japan?" — a question we often receive from customers. The answer is: "Yes, since 2018." In this column, we explain the regulatory history behind that answer, as a company that held repeated consultations with Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare toward the reform.

Bovine placenta was once the mainstay

It may come as a surprise, but bovine placenta was once the center of the placenta raw-material market. When BSE (bovine spongiform encephalopathy) was confirmed in Japan in 2001, however, the environment surrounding cattle-derived materials changed completely. Under a ministry notification, cattle-derived materials were strictly regulated, bovine placenta became unusable, and the market shifted to porcine placenta.

The ban continued even after BSE-free certification

In 2013, Japan was certified BSE-free by the OIE (World Organisation for Animal Health, now WOAH). Even so, the use of bovine placenta remained banned. Once regulations are tightened, they do not automatically return even after safety is restored. Meanwhile, valuable bovine placenta continued to be discarded at slaughterhouses across the country.

February 28, 2018 — the reform arrives

Driven by the desire to make effective use of the bovine placenta being discarded nationwide, we visited the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare again and again, proposing and discussing safety and management methods. At times the Ministry itself sought our opinions and advice as we continued working toward regulatory reform.

Then, on February 28, 2018, a revision of the "Standards for Biological Raw Materials" published in the official gazette permitted the use of bovine placenta. At the same time, "cattle" was added as a placenta-extract source material in the "Japanese Standards of Quasi-Drug Ingredients 2006."

Bovine placenta today

Today, DARD manages placenta collected from cattle farms in Hokkaido entirely in-house, manufacturing bovine placenta raw material with safety as the first priority. Under a traceability system tracking production history from birth through shipment, we supply it as a raw material for cosmetic placenta extract and more.

For raw-material selection consultations, including the regulatory background, please feel free to contact us.
