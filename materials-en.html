Quality Control & Traceability

Nothing matters more for animal-derived raw materials than ensuring safety. At DARD, our own staff manage everything from collection through manufacturing and quality checks. In addition to Japan's regulatory standards, we apply our own strict in-house quality criteria.

  • Proof of domestic origin: production regions in Japan are clearly disclosed.
  • Inspection & records: inspection records for every process are retained.
  • Traceability: production history can be tracked from birth through shipment.

To secure hygienic raw materials, we have built systems that satisfy the standards set by the notifications and ordinances of Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, developed under the detailed guidance of the Ministry's evaluation, food-safety monitoring, and compliance divisions.
